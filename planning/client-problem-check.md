Client: The specific group of people that this solution is intended for are highschool students/college students. 
This solution is designed to help students focus during study sessions, highschool/college students tend to study more hours so this solution can work very well for them. 

Current situation: Many students currently handle this problem by procrastinating or saving their study time for the very last minutes. This affects a student's growth in their academics.

Problem: The specific unmet need is focus. Students are not learning as much during their study sessions due to a lack in focus. My program is designed to maximize focus. 

Proposed solution: My program will allow clients to have a timer that will allow them to track their study sessions, along with a implemented break timer that will allow the client 5-10 minute breaks. The program will include a library design, that will stimulate a focused environment. Along with the design , there will be a sound toggle that will turn on sounds like pages turning, rain sounds, etc. The most important part of my program is that there will implementation of a goal system that will allow students to track their study hours per week. If they reach their goal they gain rewarads that they can use in the app. 

Evidence needed: 

What environment helps you focus more?
What sounds help you focus?
What drives your motivation?

Success criteria: 

"The solution will allow students to stimulate a focused environment at home"
"The solution will allow students drive away from procrastination and distractions"
"The solution will help teachers/students track their "focus" hours which will ultimately help with student academics"
